## Editorial interpretation

The useful connection to the Guardian paper is the relationship between a present observation and information that affects later behavior. The Guardian's visible `Infant` label omits its consecutive-stability counter: two matching labels can therefore have different next transitions. That code example supplies its own evidence; this illustration offers a way to approach the broader idea.

Three distinctions guide reading across the collection:

- **Framework vocabulary and depicted mechanisms:** “refuse” and “collapse” are the figure's interpretive labels. The analogy alone does not establish a biological implementation of the framework.
- **Signal and record:** “committed signal” describes the figure's output. Committing a durable adjudication record in the papers is a separately specified operation; permission, execution, and recording need distinct contracts.
- **Present appearance and future-relevant history:** a history-sensitive system requires a representation of the distinctions that matter to its task. The figure alone does not show that any particular MEM8 or Guardian implementation preserves those distinctions.
