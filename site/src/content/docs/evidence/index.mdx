## One work, several representations

Each curated work has a stable catalog entry. Its source files and checksums
identify the edition; the web Reader is derived from those files. The LaTeX and
PDF remain available beside the text. Citations, equations, and qualifications
belong to the reading edition, including statements such as “proposed” and
“not yet verified.”

The existing repository collection is also searchable. Those documents retain
their original claims and have not all received the September editorial review.

## Compression and retrieval

A compressed artifact must declare whether it reconstructs its input exactly.
Lossless storage can be verified by decoding and comparing hashes. Summaries,
rewrites, and concept maps require separate fidelity checks and source links.
None replaces the original or inherits its evidential status automatically.

The Marqant and Kompress trial is recorded with a reproducible script in the
[repository’s library tools](https://github.com/8b-is/8b-public-documents/tree/main/library-tools).

## Authorship and reuse

Authorship and upstream sources stay attached to each work. Making a document
readable here does not assign it a new license. Where redistribution terms are
unspecified, consult the author before further reuse. Private chat exports are
not part of the reading collection.
