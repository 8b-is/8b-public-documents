## Guardian: from distinction to behavior

1. [Unknown Is Not Don’t-Care](/library/unknown-not-dontcare/) — distinguish missing evidence, specification freedom, and an insufficient state representation.
2. [From Vocabulary to Kernel](/library/vocabulary-to-kernel/) — examine the obligations that connect the conceptual framework to code.
3. [Guardian playground](/playground/) — step two counters through the same inputs and inspect the different outcomes.
4. [Evidence & editions](/evidence/) — check the limits of the example and the proposed next experiment.

## History, commitment, and verification

1. [Commitment Before Appearance](/library/commitment-before-appearance/) — distinguish committed history from its material and semantic projections.
2. [The Verification Boundary](/library/verification-boundary/) — examine the independence of the evidence used to evaluate a claim.
3. [Two Meanings of Fixed Point](/library/fixed-point-essay/) — separate numerical representations, equilibrium, reproducibility, and provenance.

## A visual entry point

[Field Before Spike](/library/field-before-spike/) includes the original illustration,
a full text transcription, and editorial notes on the scope of its analogy.
Continue with the Guardian reading path to see a concrete software example of
future-relevant information that a visible output alone does not capture.
