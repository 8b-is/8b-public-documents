---
title: Accessibility
description: Reading options, keyboard controls, and the current limits of the library.
---

The Reader provides web text in addition to source and PDF downloads. Use the
site’s theme control for light or dark reading, your browser’s zoom for larger
type, and the skip link to reach the main content. Equations include MathML
through KaTeX; their announcement depends on your browser and assistive technology.

The Explorer uses labeled search and topic controls. All documents remain
available when JavaScript is disabled. The Guardian playground uses standard
buttons, labeled controls, and text outcomes; JavaScript is required to run it.

The Field Before Spike illustration has a text transcription and editorial notes.
Other legacy images and PDFs may still need fuller descriptions or tagged PDF
editions. This release does not claim a completed accessibility certification.

To report a barrier, [open an issue](https://github.com/8b-is/8b-public-documents/issues)
with the page address, the control or passage involved, and the behavior you
expected. Contributions to transcripts and accessible reading editions are welcome.
